# FLEURS 01: real Hungarian speech baseline (2026-11-07)

First run on real speech: 100 clips from the Hungarian dev split of [FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) (Google, CC-BY-4.0), one recording per sentence, the first 100 sentences in file order. Fetched with `scripts/fetch-fleurs.py` into `bench/audio/fleurs-hu/` (git-ignored; never commit it). Read speech by many speakers, average 12 s per clip (5.7–30.2 s), news and encyclopedia style with many foreign names. Apple M5 Pro, idle machine, `pusmogo-bench --features whisper`, engine + cleanup, warm engine. The reference and output columns below quote FLEURS transcripts (CC-BY-4.0).

## Findings

1. **Real speech is much harder than TTS: WER 17.5 %, CER 4.5 %** (synthetic Hungarian in [synth-01](synth-01.md): WER 7.5 %, CER 1.6 %). CER stays low: most counted word errors are one or two letters off, compound splits or punctuation-adjacent tokens, not lost words. 20/100 clips are exact, 14/100 have WER ≥ 40 %.
2. **Double consonants and vowel length fail on real voices too** ("kórt" → "kort", "elni" for "élni", "síelés" → "sijelés", "megragadják" → "megagadják"), confirming the synth-01 finding 3 is not a TTS artefact.
3. **Compound splits and joins are frequent** ("kempingterületekkel" → "kemping területekkel", "felsőoktatásba" → "felső oktatásba", "közel merészkednek" → "közelmerészkednek"). Each costs two word errors for one space; scoring them separately (bench normaliser item) would make WER more honest.
4. **Foreign proper names dominate the worst clips** ("Vinson" → "Winston", "Uthappa" → "utappa", "Chhappan Bhogot" → "csappán bogot", "Biskek" → "Biszrek"). Typical dictation has fewer of these than FLEURS; a user dictionary (D1/C6) is the fix for names that matter.
5. **The audio beyond 30 s hallucinates.** The one 30.2 s clip ends in "NAMASTE": Whisper decodes the 0.2 s tail as a second window, the same output as on silence in synth-01. The hallucination guard must also cover short tail windows, and streaming (B2) should split long dictation at pauses instead of at 30 s.
6. **Latency barely grows with length:** median 0.76 s for clips under 8 s, 0.80 s for 8–12 s, 0.83 s for 12–16 s, 0.85 s for 16–30 s (RTF 0.07). The fixed 30 s encoder window dominates, as in synth-01; B3 (`audio_ctx` for short clips) targets the short end.

Engine `whisper` (`ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin`), load 0.21 s, cold first call 0.77 s (excluded below).
